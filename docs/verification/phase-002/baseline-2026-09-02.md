## Issue evidence

The prior repair commit `404c011` addressed adoption of already materialized durable stock, but the later report still described an unavailable offer and missing money or items after adding gunpowder. The current phase treats that result as unresolved until a local transition and current state matrix explains it.

Issue 32 reports two unusable player data incidents requiring deletion. The report does not identify a trigger, file, field, or matching FutureShops state. The phase will generate bounded disposable states and preserve ownership sentinels. Reporter files are not required.

Issue 34 reports that finite stock money purchases fail while infinite stock purchases succeed on multiplayer, with no useful log entry. The phase will compare both paths on an isolated dedicated Forge server with two independent clients and capture the first authoritative divergence.
