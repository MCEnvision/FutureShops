## Economy ownership and call graph

CodeGraph identified the economy boundary and its blast radius. `BalanceManager` owns the process-wide provider reference, initializes `InternalEconomyProvider` on `ServerStartingEvent`, clears it on `ServerStoppingEvent`, and rejects access before initialization. `InternalEconomyProvider` owns balance reads and checked deposit, withdrawal, transfer, and leaderboard operations. It persists balances through overworld `InternalBalanceSavedData`, whose schema is version 1. `EconomyProvider` is the current interface for balance, deposit, withdrawal, transfer, leaderboard, currency name, and decimal places. `TransactionResult` carries operation outcomes.

The complete direct consumer set is:

* lifecycle: `Futureshops.java`
* public API: `api/ShopModAPI.java`
* commands: `BalTopCommand.java`, `BalanceCommand.java`, `DepositCommand.java`, `PayCommand.java`, `ShopAdminCommand.java`, and `WithdrawCommand.java`
* physical currency: `money/MoneyItem.java`
* shop and analytics services: `server/shop/MarketplaceAnalyticsService.java`, `PlayerShopBlockService.java`, and `ShopDataService.java`
* transactions: `server/transaction/ShopBuyService.java` and `ShopSellService.java`

The economy declarations and persistence files are `server/economy/BalanceManager.java`, `EconomyProvider.java`, `InternalEconomyProvider.java`, `InternalBalanceSavedData.java`, and `TransactionResult.java`. The call graph includes balance queries, leaderboard reads, player payments, administration, deposits, withdrawals, physical money redemption, server shop buy and sell flows, player shop settlement and claims, analytics, and lifecycle initialization and cleanup. The current graph contains no external provider registry or provider selection boundary.
